Zero-Storage Privacy Proof

Why this won't leak sensitive citizen records: Government certificates (such as caste affidavits, Aadhaar numbers, and income tax statements) are high-risk targets for identity theft.

Architecture Guarantees:
  • No Backend Database: No PostgreSQL, Mongo, or cloud storage bucket stores applicant identity.
  • Client-Side FileReader: Files dropped into the scanner are parsed as local browser memory buffers using regular expressions.
  • Garbage Collection: As soon as the modal is closed or tab is refreshed, the buffer in RAM is cleared automatically.
  • No Third-Party AI APIs: Sensitive PDF text is never forwarded to OpenAI or external LLMs.
